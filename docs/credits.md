The editable `StockPlayerList` and `StockMobilePlayerList` instance trees capture Roblox’s desktop and mobile player-list layouts. The Luau renderer binds fresh game data to those templates. The source fallback reconstructs their layout when the editable templates are absent.

Builder Sans, Builder Icons, stock button imagery and avatar thumbnails are referenced through Roblox’s asset system. The screenshots and GIFs show the module running in Roblox Studio.
